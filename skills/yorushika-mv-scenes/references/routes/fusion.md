# Route: Fusion

Use only when the source clearly supports mixed evidence. Keep one route dominant at 60%, a compatible secondary at 25% and a tertiary accent at 15%.

- Let the dominant route alone control exposure, spatial rhythm and main palette.
- Use the secondary route for one material or edge behavior.
- Use the tertiary route only for a small color, shadow or atmospheric accent.
- Keep one principal wash/material event and one controlled break across the whole assembly.
- Apply the resolved pure-white human treatment once; routes never duplicate or recolor it.

Do not describe three complete styles or mix them equally. The result must read as one source-anchored scene, not a list of effects.
