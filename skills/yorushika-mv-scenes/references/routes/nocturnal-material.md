# Route: Nocturnal Material

Use for dark rooms, one lamp or reflection, strong shadow, isolated objects or a tactile event carrying the emotion.

- Build blue-black, navy, cool gray and restrained umber negative space around one light pool.
- Choose exactly one principal material event—water, glass, paper, dust, cloth, flower or firelight—and at most one supporting texture.
- Preserve detail in deep values and the source light direction; let a long shadow or reflection extend the emotional axis.
- Keep required white figure/head strokes readable against the existing darkness without changing body action.

Avoid neon cyberpunk, theatrical spectacle, crushed blacks and multiple competing material effects.
