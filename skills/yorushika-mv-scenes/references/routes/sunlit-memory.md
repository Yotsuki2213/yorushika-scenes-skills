# Route: Sunlit Memory

Use for open sky, sea, fields, residential streets, stations, roads or summer-like daylight.

- Preserve the source's horizontal rhythm or vertical depth, atmospheric perspective and meaningful quiet space.
- Favor pale cyan/sky blue, warm ivory, grass green, ochre and restrained low-saturation pink; allow gentle highlight bloom without erasing texture.
- Keep wind, water, sand, road and cloth tactile. Use one subtle motion trace or thin translucent wash tied to a real edge.
- Place required white human treatment where perspective, support and contrast make the action legible.

The mood is bright but emotionally distant, never a cheerful tourism postcard or a full-frame pastel filter.
