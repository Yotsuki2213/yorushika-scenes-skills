# Human Treatment Router

Resolve the branch from the source before reading details. A human subject carries compositional or narrative focus through scale, isolation, action, gaze or placement; distant incidental passers-by alone do not qualify.

- No human subject: read [add-white-protagonist](humans/add-white-protagonist.md).
- Existing primary human subject(s): read [cover-existing-heads](humans/cover-existing-heads.md).

Both branches use pure-white, uneven hand-drawn strokes and dense full-head anonymity. The route and orientation do not change the branch. Existing bodies remain protected; visible heads to cover or a new figure footprint are authorized edit zones.

For either branch, inspect the bundled [contact sheet](../assets/line-figures/线稿参考联系表.png) as the default supporting style reference. It summarizes the three unchanged originals listed in the [source record](../assets/line-figures/SOURCES.md). Extract only contour wobble, sparse body hatching, dense horizontal head scribbles and believable scene contact. Large white preparation masks and reference backgrounds are not art direction.

Attach the user's scene as `EDIT TARGET` and the contact sheet as `SUPPORTING LINE STYLE ONLY`. Use one original reference only for a special action the contact sheet cannot show; never attach the contact sheet and all three originals together.
