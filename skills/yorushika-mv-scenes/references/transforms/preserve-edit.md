# Transform: Preserve Edit

The supplied image remains visibly present as the edit target.

- `strict`: keep roughly 80–90% of source structure/pixels; allow required human regions, local accents, necessary natural frame extension and requested local repair.
- `balanced`: keep principal subjects and roughly 60–80% of structure, spatial relations and texture; allow bounded simplification and one source-anchored graphic field.
- `loose`: keep primary subjects, body anchors and semantic minimum while allowing broader environmental editing; it remains an edit.

Lock viewpoint, existing bodies/clothing/poses, principal objects, dominant geometry, light direction and foreground material. Exclude the chosen visible-head regions or new figure footprint from those locks. Percentages are art-direction estimates, not measured guarantees.
