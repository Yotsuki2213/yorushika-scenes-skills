# Cover Existing Heads

Use when one or more people carry the source's composition or narrative. Preserve every primary subject's body, age, clothing, footwear, pose, relative scale, placement and contact. Apply only the anonymous-head language from [human treatment](../human-treatment.md); do not impose a scene-integration-master pose, barefoot rule or body treatment, and do not add another person by default.

- Mark each entire visible head as an authorized edit even under `strict`.
- Cover crown, hair, face, ears and back of head until no identity detail reads through; keep the shared rough hand-scrawled character while protecting neck/body landmarks and adjacent important objects.
- Preserve the head's overall scale and facing gesture. For partial heads, cover only the visible region and respect occlusion.
- Apply separately to all co-primary subjects, including back-facing people. Do not invent a completely hidden or off-frame head.

In `redraw`, unlocked surroundings may change, but body anchors, original footwear and head-treatment decisions remain fixed unless the user explicitly releases them. The barefoot rule applies only to a newly added figure in the no-human-subject branch.
