# Cover Existing Heads

Use when one or more people carry the source's composition or narrative. Preserve every primary subject's body, clothing, pose, relative scale, placement and contact. Do not impose the new-protagonist back-facing/look-back pose and do not add another person by default.

- Mark each entire visible head as an authorized edit even under `strict`.
- Cover crown, hair, face, ears and back of head with dense, irregular pure-white hatching and overlapping mostly horizontal scribbles until no identity detail reads through.
- Keep the cover visibly hand-scrawled, not a smooth fill. Stroke ends may extend slightly beyond the head while protecting neck/body landmarks and adjacent important objects.
- Preserve the head's overall scale and facing gesture. For partial heads, cover only the visible region and respect occlusion.
- Apply separately to all co-primary subjects, including back-facing people. Do not invent a completely hidden or off-frame head.

In `redraw`, unlocked surroundings may change, but body anchors and head-treatment decisions remain fixed unless the user explicitly releases them.
