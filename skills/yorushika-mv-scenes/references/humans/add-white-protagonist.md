# Add White Protagonist

Use only when no person carries the source's composition or narrative. Add one anonymous, legible protagonist in uneven pure-white ink, pencil or chalk-like contours and hatching.

- Choose scale and placement from perspective, eye path and emotional weight; preserve principal objects and depth.
- Choose the figure's action from the scene first; the default is one believable still with torso facing away and the head turning naturally back over one shoulder, with a small coordinated upper-torso turn if needed. When that default contradicts the scene—its sightlines, path, support or narrative—adapt the action freely while keeping it equally grounded and plausible. Never use a backward-mounted face, impossible neck twist or front-facing chest on a back-facing pelvis.
- Ground the figure on visible support: a weight-bearing walking foot, seated pelvis, or plausible hand/forearm contact while leaning. Respect joints, gravity, balance, occlusion and foreground depth; do not float in sky or water or invent a conspicuous prop.
- Keep the body slightly abstract through wobbling broken contours, sparse overlapping construction lines and transparent hatching gaps, while torso, limbs, support and turn remain readable.
- Cover the entire visible head—crown, hair mass, face, ears and back—with dense irregular white hatching and mostly horizontal scribbles. No features may read through; avoid a smooth disk, empty oval or censor rectangle.

The route may adjust nearby tone within a bounded region for white-line visibility, but it must not replace the scene or import a reference pose/background.
