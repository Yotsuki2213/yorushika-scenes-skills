# Add White Protagonist

Use only when no person carries the source's composition or narrative. Apply the shared line grammar from [human treatment](../human-treatment.md) to one anonymous, completely barefoot late-teen or young-adult youth or girl.

- Choose scale and placement from perspective, eye path and emotional weight; preserve principal objects and depth.
- Choose the figure's action from the scene's path, support, edge, sightline and emotional distance. Use a believable back-facing look-back only when the scene provides no stronger cue. Never use a backward-mounted face, impossible neck twist or front-facing chest on a back-facing pelvis.
- Ground the figure on visible support: a weight-bearing bare foot, seated pelvis, or plausible hand/forearm contact while leaning. Draw each bare foot as only a few coarse broken marks—no toes, soles, socks, shoes or sandals. Respect joints, gravity, balance, occlusion and foreground depth; do not float in sky or water or invent a conspicuous prop.
- Keep the youthful body and simple clothing subordinate to the gesture; do not refine it into fashion illustration, finished manga line art or academic figure drawing.

The route may adjust nearby tone within a bounded region for white-line visibility, but it must not replace the scene or import the action master's exact pose, outfit, support diagram or background.
