# Optional Composition & Expression Pass

Read this only when the user requests a detailed analysis or when the compact Scene Card cannot resolve a difficult composition. Normal generation uses the ten fields in `SKILL.md`.

## Evidence pass

Describe only visible evidence before style:

- the one or two forms that identify the scene;
- horizon, path, architecture, overlap, facing direction and relative scale;
- existing body action, contact and every visible head region;
- dominant light/dark masses, texture density and useful quiet space;
- the main line, curve, convergence, gaze or movement carrying the eye;
- one source contour, plane, shadow or material event that can support intervention;
- one concise emotional tension grounded in the image.

Do not count distant incidental passers-by as primary human subjects. Required head regions or a new figure footprint remain editable; existing bodies and all other scene anchors remain locked according to the selected transform mode.

## Abstraction decision

For a difficult editable area, decide only:

- `retain`: defining forms and relations;
- `merge`: repeated micro-detail into a quiet mass;
- `omit`: nonessential clutter only when the transform mode permits;
- `transform`: selected edges into ink, watercolor or displaced print;
- `expose`: negative space that lets the frame breathe.

Strong style means a stronger connected intervention on selected source shapes, not replacement of the semantic minimum. Place one primary event on the source's dominant gesture, then let the eye leave through quiet space, a fading wash or an unfinished contour.

## Optional expression sentence

For an ambiguous source, record one internal sentence:

```text
The scene expresses [tension] through [gesture/material], so the selected treatment follows [source axis] while [hard locks] remain intact.
```

Revise the proposed figure placement if its action, support or visibility cannot be grounded in the scene.
